# Services

We help engineering teams ship software confidently through structured QA, agentic test tooling, and quality signal infrastructure.

## What we do

- **QA foundations** — baseline coverage, CI harness, test architecture from scratch or rebuild
- **Agentic QA** — AI-assisted test generation, autonomous regression coverage, LLM-evaluated quality gates
- **Quality signal infrastructure** — dashboards, release-confidence scores, cross-team visibility into what is actually tested and what is not
- **Audit and triage** — one-time deep-dives into why deploys are risky, what the coverage map looks like, and a prioritized fix list

## Engagements

| Type | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Audit** | A single deep-dive: coverage map, risk analysis, prioritized findings report |
| **Retainer** | Ongoing embedded QA engineering, async and sprint-aligned |
| **Platform** | Access to qulib — our open-source MCP that integrates QA signal into your existing toolchain |

## How to request services

The fastest path is a GitHub issue using our intake form:

**[Submit a service request](https://github.com/TapeshN/tapeshnagarwal.com/issues/new?template=service-intake.yml)**

Fill in what you can — company name, contact email, and a description of the problem are enough to start a conversation. We will reply within 2 business days.

Alternatively, email **tapeshnagarwal@gmail.com** with the subject line `[Intake] <your company>`.

## What happens after you submit

1. We review your request within 2 business days.
2. If it looks like a fit, we schedule a short scoping call (30 min, no obligation).
3. We send a proposal with scope, timeline, and pricing.
4. You decide. No pressure, no retainer lock-in on the first engagement.
